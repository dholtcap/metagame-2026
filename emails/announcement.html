Metagame 2026
Nov 6–8  ·  Berkeley, CA  ·  Lighthaven Campus
Metagame is a weekend conference devoted to games. We mean games in the broadest sense of the word: any experience that is designed to be played, as opposed to passively consumed. If building it involves asking the question “what would the consumer of this experience choose to do next?”, it’s a game.
